Automatic Sync

While on, visiting the Change Management tab re-syncs in the background once this interval has passed — at most once per interval, however many people visit. For a schedule that doesn't depend on someone visiting the page, point an external daily cron (Render Cron Job, GitHub Actions, etc.) at python manage.py sync_azure_devops instead.
